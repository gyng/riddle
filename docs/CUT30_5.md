# Cut 30.5 — the works tree: start manual, automate

*Contract, 2026-10-02. Design: `docs/AUTOMATION_TREE.md` (branch `cut30-client`, `9414d2b`). The owner's answers to its §7
(2026-10-02): (1) build it now, as Cut 30.5, before the Cut 30 owner check and cohort 25; (2) the first worker, the porter,
is free, the rest cost gold; (3) **manual send first**, against the doc's recommendation: the first hero's send is a tap
until a send worker is hired, and the owner chose it knowing offline progress pauses until then; (4) the works tree
replaces the tracks panel. Base: `cut30-client` (core = `cut30` 58e7a96 + art, fonts, death v2, the town). Branches:
`c305-core` (this half) ∥ the client half, meeting at the wire (`web/src/engine/{types,fake}.ts`).*

## 1. What changes from the design doc

The doc stands except where answer (3) bends it:

- **A send worker, the scout**, joins the trunk after the porter: `quartermaster (given) → porter (free) → scout → armourer
  → apprentice`, then the branches. The doc's "deliberately not nodes: auto-send" is reversed.
- **Before the scout, a send is one run.** SEND (the gem or the mouth) sends the hero: he goes down, plays the run out
  (watched, or as the usual offline slice), comes home and **waits**. Nothing sends him again: an absence before the
  hire yields at most the run in flight (`tree.waits` on the wire). From the hire on, everything is as Cut 30: he goes
  down after each rest, offline is uncapped, and the gem stays *go now*.
- **The scout lands in the first session.** Trigger: 3 sends by hand; price 0.5 forge unit (~$75–100 at the first
  floors), paid from the purse then the chest (a worker takes his wage from the haul, collected or not: IDLE, who never
  opens the chest, can still hire him). Target: ≤ 15 min of play and ≤ 5 manual sends on every seed.
- **"IDLE is byte-identical" no longer holds for the first minutes.** IDLE now taps SEND until the scout lights and hires
  him at once (its only engagement), then never taps again. Its runs 1–3 come back to back instead of 20 minutes apart,
  so its fortnight is a bounded delta from Cut 30's, not a copy (§4 gates the bound). The chest itself is still
  sim-neutral: IDLE with the chest opened at every check-in is byte-identical to IDLE.

### 1.1 Owner decision, 2026-10-02: early runs go deeper while healthy (superseded by §1.3)

A first run banking at D2–D3 at near-full hp read as broken to the owner (c30-legible's probe: 37 of 50 early runs
end on Steady's `depth ≥ best + 1 → bank` with its D2 floor). **While the record is under D8, Steady banks two floors
past the record when whole (hp ≥ 60 %) and at the record's next floor when hurt** — the L4 shape from L1
(`packages::EARLY_DEEPER_UNTIL`). The exit reasons stay accurate: a bank row at the record's next floor reads `banks
every record`, a whole hero's bank a floor further reads `new best · banked`, the hurt row `hurt · banked`
(`engine::exit_reason`). Re-measured with the gates (§4; Status).

### 1.2 Owner decisions, 2026-10-02: automation pays the away player; week-2 content

- **"Automation pays" is measured on an away player**: AWAY (PICKED at one check-in a day, its workers hired) against
  AWAY − nodes (the same bot doing every chore by hand at its one check-in, the trunk to the scout hired) — AWAY reaches
  D18 sooner on the median seed, or holds a deeper mean best over the fortnight; the same 16 seeds as the other rows.
  The 3×/day player keeps "never slower" (HANDS vs IDLE). (At 3 check-ins a day PICKED vs PICKED − nodes tied at D18,
  32 vs 32 h: the chores by hand at each check-in leave the workers only the hours inside an absence.)
- **Week-2 content** so PICKED's days with a stage opened hold ≥ 10/14 at 16 seeds (aim ~11), the bar unchanged:
  - **worker ranks** (the owner's "later worker upgrades or looks"): a hired worker's rank II, III, IV come 4, 8 and 12
    days of service after its hire (`tree::RANK_DAYS`), each a forge unit × the rank less one, one on offer at a time
    when no hire is lit (`promote(id)`; the `next` pill names it, `apprentice II · $175`): the worker's look and its
    post's on the town scene, a town stage (`+apprentice II`); no effect on the sim.
  - **a wall wears down**: each band boss's scars at ×3 and at the cap ×6 (days met) are scale stages — the progress a
    held record makes while it holds (`Queen scarred ×3`).
  - **the guide keeps a ledger**: per waystone, its recent sends and those that brought gold home; it starts at the
    deepest lit stone a band under the record that pays, gives a stone up when half its sends come home empty, and
    lets a start picked by hand stand while it pays and the record holds. (Found tuning: the bare "a band under the
    record" rule sent PICKED from D24 into the Queen's band, every send home with $0 from day 5 on — a content bug.)
  - **the armourer brings a find only when it beats the forged kit** (a weaker one rode in the pack and took a find's
    slot: AWAY with the armourer read a mean best 24.3 vs 25.9 by hand).

### 1.3 Owner decision, 2026-10-02: a new record never ends the run

"Reaching a new depth seems to end the run, which seems unsatisfying." **A new record is a beat (`new best · D5`, a
callout) and a checkpoint, never an exit.** The record is kept; **the gold carried so far is secured** — each new
deepest floor past the record the run left with moves the carry into `Run::secured`, which comes home whole at any
exit, a death's included (as a waystone holds what it has seen). The exit economy stays bank 100 % · return 60 % ·
death 0 % of the carry *since the last checkpoint* (the code's death share is 0: the 30 % in the docs is the heir's
purse floor), plus the secured gold. A death stays meaningful: the heir is lost, the carry since the checkpoint is lost,
the floor's finds go to the bones. The exit line leads with it (`banked $120 · $80 secured + 100% of $40`, `died $80 ·
$41 lost`); `ExitLine.secured` on the wire; qa's "a death keeps nothing" reads "nothing but its secured gold".

A run now ends only when the hero is hurt (the stance's hp rule: a bank once past the record, a return before it), out
of heals (`lacks heal`, a new condition: a bank), at a wall he cannot pass (a drive-off, a stall), or dead. This
supersedes Steady's bank-at-record rows and §1.1's "deeper when healthy". The stances keep their characters: Steady
goes home hurt under 25 % (out of heals under 40 %, L4 35 %), Guarded under 30 % (out of heals 45 %) and rests between
fights, Hunter under 20 % with the boss and the summoned first, Bold never turns back before the record and banks past
it under 25 %. Tuned on the rows: Steady at 40 % held IDLE under D8 on day 1 on every seed (25 % gives D8 16/16, D13
1.7 d, D23 med 5.7 d); Guarded at 45 % was the picker's choice and PICKED crawled (D18 1.07× IDLE; at 30 %: 1.65×). Exit
reasons: `hurt · banked`, `hurt · went home`, `no heals · banked`; `banks every record` is gone with the rows that
made it. The replay hash is re-recorded (the save's own set: only the secured gold moves).

## 2. The tree (core: `crates/riddle-core/src/tree.rs`)

| # | Node | Branch | Chore by hand (the call) | Trigger · fallback | Price (forge units) | Worker's standing order |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | quartermaster | trunk | — | given | — | packs the heal and a drill's item (the restock, as Cut 30) |
| 1 | porter | trunk | open the chest (`openChest`) | 3 chests | **free** | hauls land in the purse (no chest) |
| 2 | **scout** | trunk | SEND (`send`) | 3 sends | 0.5 | the hero goes down after each rest (offline uncapped) |
| 3 | armourer | trunk | wear a find (`loadout` with a vault item) | 2 finds worn · 24 h | 1 | each send brings the best vault weapon and armour |
| 4 | apprentice | trunk | a forge step (`buyKit`) | 3 steps · 30 h | 3 | at each send, the cheapest next step the purse pays keeping 3 units (the shelf's money) |
| 5 | keeper | items | a keep sheet answered / a storehouse sale (`keep`, `sellVault`) | 2 · 30 h | 2 | a watched exit never asks: the keep order settles it |
| 6 | clerk | town | a deposit (`bankDeposit`) | 3 · 36 h | 4 | at each send, the purse above the next forge step + 3 units into the bank (to its cap) |
| 7 | drillmaster | character | a level bought (`spendLevel`) | 2 · 36 h | 3 | at each send, marks into the worn stance's next level, then the tactics' |
| 8 | kennel-hand | town | field a pet (`setParty`, `hatch`) | 2 · 40 h | 3 | at each send, empty party slots filled with the kennel's best |
| 9 | herald | town | swap the quest (`swapQuest`) | 2 · 40 h | 2 | a quest drawn on an earlier day and still unkept is swapped (the day's free swap) |
| 10 | guide | scale | pick a start (`setStart`) | 3 · 44 h | 4 | at each send, the deepest lit stone a band (4 floors) under the record, never shallower than the start set |

- A node's **chore exists** once its system is open (`storehouse`, `forge`, `bank`, `kennel`, `quests`, `start`); until
  then it is `shut`. It is **ready** at its count or, its chore open, at its fallback age (PROGRESSION_V2 §4's rule 3:
  a fallback for every skill trigger, so a chore a player never meets still gets its worker by day 2).
- **One node lit at a time**: the first ready, unhired node in the order above is `lit` (the one BUY); a ready node
  behind it waits `ready`. A hire is refused unless the node is lit.
- **Prices in the forge unit** (`kit::unit_of`, fixed when the forge is first shown): tenths of a unit, rounded.
- Workers act only between a lineage's real runs (`Game::start_run`, not in sims) and never on a literal (harness)
  lineage. Each act is counted (`ReturnReport.workers`, `first` once per worker). A hired worker can be switched off
  (`setWorker(id, false)`): its chore is by hand again (the scout off: sends by hand again).
- **The chest** (`tree.chest`, the haul not yet collected) is part of the lineage's gold: `Lineage.gold` on the wire is
  the purse (collected), the chest is beside it. Exits, salvage and passage coins go to the chest until the porter;
  the engine's needs (restock, insurance, the toll) draw on the purse, then the chest; manual buys (forge, bank,
  unlocks for gold, commissions) only on the purse. It never caps, never decays. **Conservation**: purse + chest + bank =
  `tree.ledger` (every gold movement summed, interest included) at every point.
- **The tree replaces `Lineage.tracks`**: the four tracks' stages are the tree's `stage` nodes on their branches
  (`<track>:<stage>`); `tracks` stays on the wire until the client half drops its panel, then goes. `ReturnReport.grew`
  is unchanged.
- **Old saves** (no `tree`): the quartermaster, porter and scout are pre-hired, the ledger opened at purse + bank — no
  player regresses (a send stays automatic, no haul waits in a chest).

## 6. Deviations

- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02), answer (3): manual send first.** The invariant *"Offline is uncapped; nothing punishes
  absence"* becomes *"Offline is uncapped once the send worker is hired (≤ ~15 min of play); before that a send is one
  run."* This is a behaviour the owner chose, not a gate weakened: before the scout an absence yields one run where Cut 30
  yielded ~20 an 8 h night. The AGENTS.md and PLAN.md edits are made when the gates pass (§7).
- **IDLE's byte-identity (the doc's §5.1/§5.2 row) is replaced by the bounded-delta row** (§4): IDLE must tap SEND
  three times and hire the scout. The chest's own neutrality stays byte-identical.
- **The guide's start is a rule, not a forecast**: the deepest lit stone 4 floors under the record (the doc: the deepest
  stone the stance survives at ≥ 50 %). A forecast at every send costs ~1 s in wasm and minutes of gate CPU.
- **The keeper's chore includes a storehouse sale** (`sellVault`) beside the keep sheet, and every node has a fallback
  age: the keep sheet appears only at a watched exit with a full storehouse, which a player who watches little never
  meets.
- **Hires draw on the chest** after the purse (manual forge, bank and gold-unlock buys draw on the purse alone).
- **The trunk to the scout is every bot's.** A bot that opens the chest (HANDS, PICKED − nodes) lights the free porter
  first, in the scout's way (one lit at a time), so it hires the porter too; HANDS otherwise never buys a node.
- **PICKED and TUNED share one first session (45 min)**, not V2's 45 and 90: TUNED's prefix is PICKED's until the pen,
  as in Cut 30's dayplayer (the shared-prefix machinery), and the pen is days away.
- **A hired worker is a town stage** (`+apprentice` in `town::stage_set`, a `grew` line on the town track): the tree
  is the tracks panel's successor, so its nodes are stages.
- **Worker tips** are the works sheet's ≤ 4-word fragments (`hauls home · while away`), the client's (c305-client
  b177393), not AUTOMATION_TREE's ≤ 10-word sentences.

- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02): workers pay is measured on the away player.** AWAY (PICKED looking in every 2–3 days —
  days 0, 3, 5, 8, 10, 13) with its workers against AWAY − nodes doing every chore by hand at those check-ins: D18 sooner
  on the median seed or a deeper mean best over 14 days. A second row for the daily player (PICKED at one check-in a
  day): the workers never cost more than one floor of mean best depth against every chore by hand. Replaces "PICKED
  reaches D18 sooner than PICKED − nodes" (3 check-ins a day: 32 vs 32 h — the chores by hand at each check-in leave
  the workers only the hours inside an absence; printed as info).
- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02): the worker edge.** Workers act at each hour of an absence (and at its end) as at a
  send; ranks give small real bonuses — porter +2 % hauls a rank, scout −5 % rest, apprentice −5 % on its steps, clerk
  +0.25 % interest a night, guide half then no toll, drillmaster a mark off a level, armourer insures what it brings
  (and so brings it at a wall too); keeper, kennel-hand and herald ranks are looks. Ranks stay optional (IDLE never
  buys one; nothing required holds without them). `WorkNode.bonus`, `rank_adds` on the wire.
- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02): RANDOM vs PICKED under the record rule** reads *RANDOM slower than PICKED on the median
  seed and on ≥ 14/16 seeds, at D13 and D23* (was every seed). With a new record no longer ending a run, a lucky
  lineage dives a band in one absence (RANDOM seed 7: D18 → D23 in 8 h, PICKED 96 h). Tried first, the owner's (a):
  the picker re-reading the panels at every check-in the record held under a band boss (no move), and a better read of
  depth (below). The every-seed form prints as retired.
- **Bot changes (Cut 30.5):** PICKED weighs a move by `pick_score` = the camp's score + 0.2 × the move in mean depth
  (the camp weighs a floor at 0.1): under the record rule how deep the sends go is the climb's pace. The first session
  (45 min) for PICKED and TUNED; HANDS; AWAY / DAILY and their by-hand twins; IDLE30 (IDLE with an old save's workers
  and no session, the bounded-delta reference — under the record rule it is no longer cut30-client's IDLE, the rule
  having moved both).

- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02): the D5 lanes row (IDLE) compares each lane reaching D8, the band's end**, not
  passing the Warlord there (same bar: the weaker at least half the stronger). Under the record rule no sim from
  IDLE's D7 record passed him on either lane, so the old row compared two zeros.
- **The stone's passage counts as a checkpoint** (the owner, 2026-10-02): a send from a lit waystone is paid the gold
  a send from D1 brings home from the floors above — an arrival's whole carry (secured with the carry since), an
  earlier end's kept gold — whatever share clears them (was: the arrivals' mean carry, nothing unless every floor
  above cleared ≥ 95 %, so under the record rule four stone sends in five were paid nothing).

- **Waystone gold accounting (2026-10-02):** the paid sends exclude the identical D1 prefix before
  the twins first diverge, so their D1 reference now excludes that prefix too. Full-lineage rates remain
  printed; the gate still requires waystone net gold per hour ≥ D1, on every eligible cohort set.
  The old comparison mixed the waystone's later segment with D1's whole history, including its unusually
  profitable early record checkpoints. The 8-seed probe passed 32/32 on matched segments (25/32 on the
  mismatched reference), without changing passage coins.

- **D5 lane content (2026-10-02):** the shallow Burrows gas lock keeps a bloat on each door but
  drops the additional corridor reserves; D8 and all later locks retain them. This reduces unavoidable
  repeated gas exposure before the first band boss, preserving the door barrier and every lane gate.
  Fresh IDLE snapshots on 8 seeds × 48 paired sends reach D8 on 31/384 near and 20/384 far
  (weaker/stronger 0.645, bar 0.5). Smaller goblin/archer groups did not help and were reverted.
