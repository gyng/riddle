# Cut 30.5 — the works tree: start manual, automate

*Contract, 2026-10-02. Design: `docs/AUTOMATION_TREE.md` (branch `cut30-client`, `9414d2b`). The owner's answers to its §7
(2026-10-02): (1) build it now, as Cut 30.5, before the Cut 30 owner check and cohort 25; (2) the first worker, the porter,
is free, the rest cost gold; (3) **manual send first**, against the doc's recommendation: the first hero's send is a tap
until a send worker is hired, and the owner chose it knowing offline progress pauses until then; (4) the works tree
replaces the tracks panel. Base: `cut30-client` (core = `cut30` 58e7a96 + art, fonts, death v2, the town). Branches:
`c305-core` (this half) ∥ the client half, meeting at the wire (`web/src/engine/{types,fake}.ts`).*

## 1. What changes from the design doc

The doc stands except where answer (3) bends it:

- **A send worker, the scout**, joins the trunk after the porter: `quartermaster (given) → porter (free) → scout → armourer
  → apprentice`, then the branches. The doc's "deliberately not nodes: auto-send" is reversed.
- **Before the scout, a send is one run.** SEND (the gem or the mouth) sends the hero: he goes down, plays the run out
  (watched, or as the usual offline slice), comes home and **waits**. Nothing sends him again: an absence before the
  hire yields at most the run in flight (`tree.waits` on the wire). From the hire on, everything is as Cut 30: he goes
  down after each rest, offline is uncapped, and the gem stays *go now*.
- **The scout lands in the first session.** Trigger: 3 sends by hand; price 0.5 forge unit (~$75–100 at the first
  floors), paid from the purse then the chest (a worker takes his wage from the haul, collected or not: IDLE, who never
  opens the chest, can still hire him). Target: ≤ 15 min of play and ≤ 5 manual sends on every seed.
- **"IDLE is byte-identical" no longer holds for the first minutes.** IDLE now taps SEND until the scout lights and hires
  him at once (its only engagement), then never taps again. Its runs 1–3 come back to back instead of 20 minutes apart,
  so its fortnight is a bounded delta from Cut 30's, not a copy (§4 gates the bound). The chest itself is still
  sim-neutral: IDLE with the chest opened at every check-in is byte-identical to IDLE.

- A node's **chore exists** once its system is open (`storehouse`, `forge`, `bank`, `kennel`, `quests`, `start`); until
  then it is `shut`. It is **ready** at its count or, its chore open, at its fallback age (PROGRESSION_V2 §4's rule 3:
  a fallback for every skill trigger, so a chore a player never meets still gets its worker by day 2).
- **One node lit at a time**: the first ready, unhired node in the order above is `lit` (the one BUY); a ready node
  behind it waits `ready`. A hire is refused unless the node is lit.
- **Prices in the forge unit** (`kit::unit_of`, fixed when the forge is first shown): tenths of a unit, rounded.
- Workers act only between a lineage's real runs (`Game::start_run`, not in sims) and never on a literal (harness)
  lineage. Each act is counted (`ReturnReport.workers`, `first` once per worker). A hired worker can be switched off
  (`setWorker(id, false)`): its chore is by hand again (the scout off: sends by hand again).
- **The chest** (`tree.chest`, the haul not yet collected) is part of the lineage's gold: `Lineage.gold` on the wire is
  the purse (collected), the chest is beside it. Exits, salvage and passage coins go to the chest until the porter;
  the engine's needs (restock, insurance, the toll) draw on the purse, then the chest; manual buys (forge, bank,
  unlocks for gold, commissions) only on the purse. It never caps, never decays. **Conservation**: purse + chest + bank =
  `tree.ledger` (every gold movement summed, interest included) at every point.
- **The tree replaces `Lineage.tracks`**: the four tracks' stages are the tree's `stage` nodes on their branches
  (`<track>:<stage>`); `tracks` stays on the wire until the client half drops its panel, then goes. `ReturnReport.grew`
  is unchanged.
- **Old saves** (no `tree`): the quartermaster, porter and scout are pre-hired, the ledger opened at purse + bank — no
  player regresses (a send stays automatic, no haul waits in a chest).

## 6. Deviations

- **Owner-approved (2026-10-02), answer (3): manual send first.** The invariant *"Offline is uncapped; nothing punishes
  absence"* becomes *"Offline is uncapped once the send worker is hired (≤ ~15 min of play); before that a send is one
  run."* This is a behaviour the owner chose, not a gate weakened: before the scout an absence yields one run where Cut 30
  yielded ~20 an 8 h night. The AGENTS.md and PLAN.md edits are made when the gates pass (§7).
- **IDLE's byte-identity (the doc's §5.1/§5.2 row) is replaced by the bounded-delta row** (§4): IDLE must tap SEND
  three times and hire the scout. The chest's own neutrality stays byte-identical.
- **The guide's start is a rule, not a forecast**: the deepest lit stone 4 floors under the record (the doc: the deepest
  stone the stance survives at ≥ 50 %). A forecast at every send costs ~1 s in wasm and minutes of gate CPU.
- **The keeper's chore includes a storehouse sale** (`sellVault`) beside the keep sheet, and every node has a fallback
  age: the keep sheet appears only at a watched exit with a full storehouse, which a player who watches little never
  meets.
- **Hires draw on the chest** after the purse (manual forge, bank and gold-unlock buys draw on the purse alone).
