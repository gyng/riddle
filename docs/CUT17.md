# Cut 17 — The frame (the game UI)

*Contract, 2026-09-24. The design authority is `docs/UI.md` (WC3/SC2 register, the IA of four
places in one frame, the skin as 9-slice sprites, the reveal ladder); the targets are
`art/ui/targets/{camp,watch,death,report}.png`; the assets are `art/ui/frames/*.png` (14) and
`art/ui/icons/*.png` (22), generated by Codex from `art/prompts/ui_{targets,frames,icons}.txt`.
Every cohort has rated feel and aesthetic 0.6 (T: 0.3).*

## Design

### 1. One frame, four wells (client)

- A persistent **top bar** (`frames/bar.png`, 9-slice 40): portrait-mini, `$`, `◆`, `★`, best
  depth, each with its icon (`icons/gold|mark|renown|depth.png`), the settings stud at the right.
- A persistent **console** (`frames/console.png`, 9-slice 96) along the bottom: the portrait well
  (`frames/well.png`, the hero's class sprite inside, an hp ring), a **command card** of up to
  4 × 2 tiles (`frames/tile.png` / `tile_pressed.png`, 9-slice 48, an icon each, a one-word label
  under), and the **primary gem** (`frames/gem.png`; `gem_red.png` for bail) with its one word.
- The **well** between them holds the place: camp (the tablets + the depth shaft), watch (the
  canvas, full bleed, no chrome over it but a thin inner bevel), death (the banner over the
  frozen floor), report (the parchment score table).
- Per place, the command card: camp `edit · loadout · unlocks · vault · forge · party · ledger ·
  chronicle` (as revealed, §3); watch `fights · fast · skip · bail` (the scrub strip along the
  console's top edge); death `edit · morgue · camp` and the gem = the top patch; report `trace ·
  gold · ledger` and the gem = `camp`.

### 2. The camp's well (client)

- Rules are **tablets** (`frames/tablet.png`; a card's row `tablet_card.png`), `R1` carved at
  the left in the display face, `cond → verb` in the middle, the verb's icon at the right; the
  editor's chips, reorder and delete move onto the tablet (a tap opens the row's editor panel).
- The forecast is the **depth shaft** at the well's right edge: one notch per depth (D1 …
  D(best+1)), lit by its reach (amber alpha = reach), the `±` as a thin halo; under it three
  gems — bank (gold), return (pale), death (red) — each with its share, and `~$N`.
- Sheets become **panels** (`frames/panel.png`, 9-slice 56, parchment body) anchored to their
  tile, each with a close stud (`frames/stud.png`).

### 3. The reveal ladder (client + core facts; `docs/UI.md` §5)

- A fresh lineage's camp: the bar with `$` only, two tablets, the shaft at D1, the gem `SEND`.
  Tiles appear on their triggers (first death → `edit`; first gold home → `loadout`; first mark
  → `unlocks` (the next three, `more` for the catalogue); an item worth keeping → `vault`; a
  salvage → `forge`; a companion → `party`; a 3rd row → the shaft's gems; a second class → the
  wake's class chips; 5 heirs → `chronicle`, `ledger`, set tabs 2–3). Each first appearance is a
  one-beat reveal (the tile glints). Triggers read the lineage (facts, gold ledger, heirs), so an
  existing save starts fully revealed.

### 4. Death and report (client)

- Death: the **banner** (`frames/banner.png`) over the dimmed floor, the cause and depth in the
  display face, the verdict in the **seal** (`frames/seal.png`); the trace on a parchment panel;
  patches as tablets with a **gauge** (`frames/gauge.png`) for `survives N %`; the gem applies
  the top patch.
- Report: a parchment panel with the tiles as engraved score plaques, the exit lines, LEARNED
  as icon chips, PLATEAU as a highlighted tablet.

### 5. Type and motion

- Display: Cinzel (titles, verdicts, R-numerals); numbers: Barlow Condensed; the pixel face
  stays for in-world callouts; monospace goes from the chrome.
- Tiles depress on tap; the gem pulses when its action waits; panels unfold (120 ms).
- Every frame has the current flat style as its fallback (art never blocks the game).
