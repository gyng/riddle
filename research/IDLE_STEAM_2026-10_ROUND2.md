**Theory.**
1. Idle moves players from playing to planning (Alharthi et al., CHI 2018) [S].
2. The pleasure is delegated, so make the delegate visible (Fizek; the Godville diary; Progress Quest).
3. A prestige formula shapes reset habits; "determine where the fun is" (Pecorella III) [F].
4. Visible new things beat currency piles (Hwang 2025) [F].
5. Show where the end is [F].
6. Care without fear of missing out, but some light stakes; with no penalties at all, attachment dropped (Hwang) [F].
7. Automation rewards a feat, not a repetition count [F].

**Tweaks to Cut 118** (applied to docs/CUT118_IDLE_LESSONS.md):
1. Boss timing: challenge tokens earned from runs (void maps), kept across heirs.
2. Trials: the last 4 weeks stay open; completable offline; the bonus is capped.
3. Finds: one reveal with the best find first; the sealed count shown on the away screen; no clicking per find.
4. Sinks: add, never upkeep; show the tithe's rate; one sink per screen.
5. Early floors: the skip is earned by clears ("3 clears → D1–7 swift") and announced.
6. Return: the one prompt shows its default; never routed through a shop.
7. Ladder: each rung names the chore it retires and the feat that earns it.
8. Crier: the hero's voice, from real trace events, short.
9. Ending: a % bar to the King, and what comes after.

**New ideas:** feats that light automation rungs (with a time fallback for IDLE); the hero's diary from the
trace; banked boss tokens; a roster preview of the next wall; upside-only requests from the hero that wait and never decay.
