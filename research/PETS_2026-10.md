**Principles.**
1. A name, deeds and one quirk create attachment; number tweaks don't.
2. Every role gets a job outside combat.
3. A pet goes "down", not "gone".
4. Breeding has one setting and one visible number.
5. Cap a pet's peak power.
6. Every synergy must fire in typical runs.
7. No randomness that feels like gambling.
