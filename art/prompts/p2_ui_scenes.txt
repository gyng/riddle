=== STYLE (docs/ART_DIRECTION.md, approved) ===
STYLE: gothic moonlit dark fantasy, a HYBRID of pixel art and painted illustration, leaning PAINTED. It looks
like a late-1980s anime-magazine watercolour illustration printed on paper, painted over a coarse visible pixel
grid: forms are blocked in chunky square pixel clusters with stepped edges (the pixel grid is visible at a
glance), but the fills inside them are transparent watercolour WASHES: soft granulation, pigment pooling darker
at the edge of each wash, slight colour bleed across a boundary, warm cream PAPER GRAIN showing through the
lightest areas, a fine HALFTONE dot screen only inside the deepest shadows (like offset print). Contours are
HAND-INKED: one confident blue-black ink line around every silhouette (thick on the shadow side, thin on the lit
side), a few fine ink hatching lines in the shadows, nothing feathered. Silhouettes are ELEGANT and LONG: tall
figures, long flowing cloaks and coats that trail and flare, sharp shapes, dramatic gesture, but each figure is
ONE bold readable mass with a clear shape against the ground (legible at 48 px tall).
PALETTE (stark and limited; name the colours, use nothing outside them except the biome tint):
  INK #0d0c14 (blue-black: outlines, the deepest shadow), UMBRA #1c1b2b (shadow), DUSK #2b3350 (mid shadow),
  MOON #4d6c99 (moonlit mid-tone), MIST #a4bcd6 (moonlit highlight), BONE #eadfc5 (paper, skin highlight, bone,
  moon), BLOOD #c01530 (THE signature accent), CLOT #5c0b1c (dark blood), EMBER #e8923a (torch flame and its
  small warm pool ONLY), GILT #b89448 (worn gold on metal trims, used sparingly).
  Per-place tint: the mid-tones (DUSK and MOON) shift toward the place's tint colour; INK, BONE and BLOOD never
  change.
VALUES: high contrast. About 60 % of the frame sits in INK/UMBRA shadow; light comes from a cold MOONLIGHT from
above (blue-white rims on the top edges of forms) and small warm EMBER torch pools. BLOOD red covers at most
~8 % of any frame and only marks what matters: the hero's cloak, blood, danger, the one primary button.
NEVER: text other than the few words asked for, logos, signatures, watermarks, photorealism, glossy 3D render,
smooth airbrushed digital gradients, lens flares, a warm brown/amber overall cast, rainbow saturation, noisy
dense detail, clean vector flat design, chibi proportions.

=== SCENE RULES ===
Painted scenes are the style at full strength: ~60 % INK/UMBRA, cold moonlight from above as shapes (shafts, rims), one or two small
EMBER pools with a visible flame, BLOOD only where the subject says. Paper grain and halftone in the shadows. No text, no UI, no
frame, no faces seen close.
ALPHA: every asset marked RGBA has a TRUE TRANSPARENT background outside the object (the four canvas corners alpha 0, no
checkerboard, no key colour, no black or paper fill). OPAQUE assets fill the canvas edge to edge.
