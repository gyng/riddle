=== WHAT THE GAME IS ===
An idle roguelike. Heroes go down into a dungeon on their own while the player is away, and come home
with gold and loot. The player never steers a hero; at home they spend gold on buildings, gear and heroes
and press SEND. The home screen is a small settlement at the mouth of the dungeon that GROWS over days of
play: a campfire and one tent on day 0, a village of a few buildings after a day, a walled town after a
week. It should feel like a GARDEN WITH THINGS MOVING: heroes walking dirt paths between buildings and the
dungeon mouth, a returning party carrying a loot sack, chimney smoke, banners, a dog, townsfolk.

=== THE STYLE ===
The SCENE (the town, filling the middle of the screen): a painted pixel-art diorama seen from a high
three-quarter top-down angle (like a classic RPG town / Warcraft III base seen from above), in the game's
WATERCOLOUR-INSPIRED PIXEL style: chunky readable pixel buildings and characters with a dark ink outline,
three tones per material, a subtle watercolour wash texture inside the fills (granulation, pigment
variation), never smooth digital gradients. Grounded earthy dark-fantasy palette: moss and olive grass,
umber dirt paths, grey stone, warm timber, thatch, oxblood and crimson banners, bone ivory, one amber
accent of firelight. Characters are small (about 1/25 of the screen height), chunky, with a readable
silhouette and a gentle anime flavour. The DUNGEON MOUTH is always the same landmark at the TOP of the
scene: a dark stone archway/stair cut into a rocky hillside, two torches, black inside. Buildings sit on
a grassy clearing, joined by dirt paths that all lead to the dungeon mouth. Each building must be
distinct in silhouette: blacksmith (open forge, anvil, glowing coals, chimney), tavern (two storeys, a
hanging sign with a mug, warm windows), bank (small stone vault with columns and a gold coin sign),
house (timber cottage), kennel (a low shed with a fenced pen and a dog), library/shrine (a small stone
tower with a blue-teal lit window and a book sign), watchtower (a tall timber tower with a flag), chronicle
hall (a long hall with banners). No text on buildings; icons only.

The FRAME (the chrome, identical in language to camp.png): Warcraft III / StarCraft II skeuomorphic,
diegetic game UI. Carved dark umber stone and blackened hammered iron with rivets and corner caps. A thin
iron RESOURCE BAR across the top with engraved icons and condensed numbers. A heavy carved stone CONSOLE
across the bottom: a round PORTRAIT WELL at the left (an anime-styled young fighter with a red cloak, a
teal health ring), a COMMAND CARD grid of square engraved icon tiles in the middle, and a big round
glowing amber GEM button at the right reading "SEND". Warm torchlight on metal edges, bevels, worn gilding.
Readable, NOT cluttered. Any text: single short words and numbers only (a carved serif like Cinzel for
words, a condensed sans for numbers), never sentences. NOT flat web design, NOT a mobile gacha UI, no
logos, no watermarks, no brand names, no tutorial text, no speech bubbles.

Small floating in-world markers are allowed (and wanted) above buildings: a tiny gold coin icon, a small
glinting "!" rune, a tiny sword icon — at most three per image.
